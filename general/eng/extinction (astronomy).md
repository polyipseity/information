## interstellar reddening

{@{Interstellar reddening}@} is {@{a phenomenon associated with interstellar extinction}@} where {@{the [spectrum](astronomical%20spectroscopy.md) of electromagnetic radiation from a [radiation source](astronomical%20object.md) changes characteristics from that which the object originally [emitted](emission%20spectrum.md)}@}. {@{Reddening}@} occurs due to {@{the light scattering off [dust](cosmic%20dust.md) and other [matter](matter.md) in the [interstellar medium](interstellar%20medium.md)}@}.
