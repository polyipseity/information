# Hooke's law

## formal definition

### linear springs

Hooke's law states that {@{$$F_s = kx$$ with $F_s$ in N being the [tension](tension%20(physics).md) and $x$ in m being the amount the coils are stretched from its equilibrium position}@}. $k$ is the {@{spring constant or stiffness in N/m}@}. <!--SR:!2032-06-25,2244,330!2029-01-09,1319,350-->

This formula states that {@{the springs exert a force proportional to the change in length}@}. This is {@{approximately true for deformations that are small compared to the entire length}@}. <!--SR:!2027-07-29,838,330!fsrs,2034-01-25T00:00:00.000Z,2650,2649.70538091,1,2,10,0,0,2026-10-24T00:00:00.000Z-->
