# five whys

__Five whys__ (or __5 whys__) is {@{an [iterative](iteration.md) interrogative [technique](skill.md) to discover [casual relationships](casuality.md) underlying a problem}@}. The technique involves {@{repeating the question "Why?" 5 or more times, directing the current "Why?" to the answer of the previous "Why?"}@}. <!--SR:!2028-02-12,1047,350!fsrs,2034-05-13T00:00:00.000Z,2711,2711.00900149,1,2,10,0,0,2026-12-10T00:00:00.000Z-->
