# chromosphere

{@{A __chromosphere__ ("sphere of color")}@} is {@{the second layer of a [star's atmosphere](stellar%20atmosphere.md)}@}, located {@{above the [photosphere](photosphere.md) and below the [solar transition region](solar%20transition%20region.md) and [corona](stellar%20corona.md)}@}. <!--SR:!2030-06-04,1556,310!fsrs,2027-07-11T04:13:12.474Z,401,401.07868927,1,2,7,0,0,2026-06-05T04:13:12.474Z!2026-12-23,227,337-->

For {@{the [Sun](Sun.md)'s chromosphere}@}, {@{the [temperature](temperature.md)}@} {@{initially decreases from the inner boundary at about 6000&nbsp;K to a minimum of approximately 3800&nbsp;K}@}, but then {@{increasing to upwards of 35&nbsp;000&nbsp;K at the outer boundary with the [transition layer](solar%20transition%20region.md) of the [corona](stellar%20corona.md)}@} \(see {@{[stellar corona § coronal heating problem](stellar%20corona.md#coronal%20heating%20problem)}@}\). <!--SR:!2027-06-28,643,250!fsrs,2029-10-12T00:00:00.000Z,1138,1137.76531831,1,2,9,0,0,2026-08-31T00:00:00.000Z!fsrs,2028-09-21T00:00:00.000Z,744,743.64694534,2.34287837,2,9,0,0,2026-09-08T00:00:00.000Z!2027-11-14,566,314!2026-11-03,294,334-->
