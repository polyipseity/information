# Zeeman energy

{@{__Zeeman energy__, or the external field energy}@}, is {@{the [potential energy](potential%20energy.md) of a magnetised body in an external [magnetic field](magnetic%20field.md)}@}. It is named after {@{the Dutch physicist [Pieter Zeeman](Pieter%20Zeeman.md)}@}, primarily known for {@{the [Zeeman effect](Zeeman%20effect.md)}@}. In {@{SI units}@}, it is given by {@{$$E_{\rm {Zeeman} }=-\mu _{0}\int _{V}\,{\textbf {M} }\cdot {\textbf {H} }_{\rm {Ext} }\,\mathrm {d} V$$}@} where {@{__H__<sub>Ext</sub> is the external field}@}, {@{__M__ the local magnetisation}@}, and the integral is {@{done over the volume of the body}@}. This is {@{the statistical average \(over a unit volume macroscopic sample\)}@} of {@{a corresponding microscopic Hamiltonial \(energy\)}@} for each {@{individual [magnetic moment](magnetic%20moment.md) __m__, which is however experiencing a _local_ induction __B__}@}: {@{$$H=-{\textbf {m} }\cdot {\textbf {B} }$$}@}
