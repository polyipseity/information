## chemistry

### reactions with metals

{@{Very dilute nitric acid}@} {@{oxidizes reactive [metals](metal.md)}@} to {@{form metal [nitrates](nitrate.md) and [hydrogen](hydrogen.md)}@}. <!--SR:!2027-12-27,1105,270!fsrs,2026-08-30T20:39:48.216Z,8,8.2956,1,2,1,0,0,2026-08-22T20:39:48.216Z!fsrs,2026-08-30T20:39:42.412Z,8,8.2956,1,2,1,0,0,2026-08-22T20:39:42.412Z-->

### reactions with nonmetals
