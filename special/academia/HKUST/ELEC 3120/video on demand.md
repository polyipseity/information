# video on demand

Video takes up most of the bytes on the Internet, and people watch more of it than they used to, in higher quality. Video splits into two kinds, and what separates them is a deadline.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- what separates on-demand video from real-time video? ::@:: Whether the next bit has to exist before playback needs it. On-demand can wait. Real-time cannot.

## on-demand video

On-demand video is recorded in advance and played when the viewer wants it, films, YouTube clips and TikToks. Nobody is waiting at the far end for the next bit to be produced. Nothing is ever late. Delivery can run ahead of playback, and the client holds what it has fetched but not yet played. That store is a buffer, and buffering is the key tool on-demand video runs on. [Progressive download](progressive%20download.md) is built on it.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- what falls under on-demand video? ::@:: Films, YouTube clips and TikToks.
- what is an on-demand buffer, in one term? ::@:: A store of data fetched ahead of playback.
- what is an on-demand client free to do, since nothing is ever late? ::@:: Fetch ahead of playback.

## real-time video

Real-time video is active communication, Skype and Zoom among them, played to the client as soon as it can possibly reach them. There is no buffer behind it, because nothing was fetched ahead of time. A bit delayed in transit arrives after the conversation has already moved on.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- why is there no buffer in real-time video? ::@:: Because the content is produced as the conversation happens, so nothing can be fetched ahead of playback.

## live streaming

A news broadcast sits between the two. It is recorded in advance, like on-demand video, but it plays to a clock. Nothing has to be produced on the spot, and yet the viewer cannot pause or skip ahead, because the schedule does not wait.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- what gives a news broadcast the squeeze it is in? ::@:: Two rules pulling opposite ways: nothing has to be produced on the spot, and the viewer still cannot pause or skip ahead.
