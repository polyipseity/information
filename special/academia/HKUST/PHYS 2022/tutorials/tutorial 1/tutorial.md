## problems

> A photon scatters off a stationary proton at an angle of $90°$. If the wavelength of the scattered photon is 25% larger than that of the incident photon, calculate:
>
> 1. The Compton wavelength of the proton ($\lambda_C$).
> 2. The initial energy of the photon ($E$).
>
> - solution: the proton's Compton wavelength is {@{$\lambda_C = h/(m_p c) \approx 1.321 \times 10^{-15}$ m (1.321 fm)}@}; the photon's initial energy is {@{$E = m_p c^2/4 \approx 234.57$ MeV}@}
> - explanation: At {@{$\theta = 90°$}@} the shift {@{$\Delta\lambda = \lambda_C(1 - \cos\theta)$ reduces to $\lambda_C$}@}. {@{The 25% increase}@} means {@{$\Delta\lambda = 0.25\lambda$, so $\lambda = 4\lambda_C$}@}.
