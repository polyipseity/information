# index

Files the PHYS 2022 notes link to. The two PDFs set the formats the course asks submissions to take. Most images were archived from Wikimedia Commons and each is embedded in the note that discusses the thing it shows; the eight drawn figures were made for these notes and regenerate byte-identically from [`generate_figures.py`](generate_figures.py).

## what each file is for

- [Aparell Franck-Hertz.png](Aparell%20Franck-Hertz.png): the Franck–Hertz tube with its three supplies — $6\ \text{V}$ filament heating, $0$ to $40\ \text{V}$ accelerating, $1.5\ \text{V}$ retarding — embedded in [Franck–Hertz experiment](../Franck%E2%80%93Hertz%20experiment.md).
- [HW sample.pdf](HW%20sample.pdf): the sample handed out for the homework, in the format submissions take.
- [Project sample.pdf](Project%20sample.pdf): the sample handed out for the mini project, in the format submissions take.
- [box_state_probability_densities.svg](box_state_probability_densities.svg): the probability density of the first three box levels, one axis per level — embedded in [particle in a box](../particle%20in%20a%20box.md).
- [box_state_wave_functions.svg](box_state_wave_functions.svg): the wave functions of the first three box levels, one axis per level, interior nodes marked — embedded in [particle in a box](../particle%20in%20a%20box.md).
- [bragg_reflection_geometry.svg](bragg_reflection_geometry.svg): the Bragg reflection construction — planes spaced $d$, glancing angle $\theta$, path difference $2d\sin\theta$ — embedded in [Bragg's law](../Bragg%27s%20law.md).
- [bragg_transmission_geometry.svg](bragg_transmission_geometry.svg): the transmission powder setup — the beam passing through the sample onto a flat plate, with the plate's face showing the central spot and concentric rings — embedded in [Bragg's law](../Bragg%27s%20law.md).
- [franck_hertz_tube.svg](franck_hertz_tube.svg): the Franck–Hertz tube with filament, grid, collector, voltmeter, electrometer, the accelerating supply running $0$ to $45\ \text{V}$ and the $1.5\ \text{V}$ retarding bias — the generated alternative rendering of the apparatus in [Franck–Hertz experiment](../Franck%E2%80%93Hertz%20experiment.md), kept alongside the embedded [Aparell Franck-Hertz.png](Aparell%20Franck-Hertz.png).
- [geiger_marsden_apparatus.svg](geiger_marsden_apparatus.svg): the Geiger–Marsden apparatus — the α source behind a lead slit, the gold foil, and the scattered beam at $\theta$ onto the screen with the microscope on the swinging arm, all inside the evacuated chamber — embedded in [Rutherford scattering experiments](../Rutherford%20scattering%20experiments.md).
- [grating_scattering_geometry.svg](grating_scattering_geometry.svg): the Davisson–Germer grating construction — the incident and scattered beams with angles $\varphi$, $\theta$, $\alpha$ and the spacings $d$ and $D$ on the lattice planes, with the straight-on direction and the $2\theta$ arc — embedded in [matter wave](../matter%20wave.md).
- [rutherford_scattering_geometry.svg](rutherford_scattering_geometry.svg): the Rutherford scattering trajectory with impact parameter $b$ and scattering angle $\theta$, embedded in [Rutherford scattering experiments](../Rutherford%20scattering%20experiments.md).
- [generate_figures.py](generate_figures.py): regenerates every drawn figure in this directory byte-identically.
