# inattentional blindness

Inattentional blindness is the failure to notice something plainly in view because attention was elsewhere. A person counting passes in a video misses someone in a gorilla suit walking through the frame. Nothing was wrong with the eyes.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- what inattentional blindness is ::@:: Failing to notice something plainly in view because attention was elsewhere.
- what the counting task demonstrates ::@:: A person counting passes misses a walker in a gorilla suit.

## prediction

Attention is predictive rather than reactive. A person anticipates the sensory, motor, and cognitive outcomes a situation usually produces, and attends to what the anticipation marked. An object nobody anticipated falls outside that set and stays unseen until someone points it out.

---

- why an unanticipated object stays unseen ::@:: It falls outside what attention predicted, and is never noticed until someone points it out.
