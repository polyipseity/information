# synesthesia

Synesthesia is the blending of two or more senses: one sense also produces a sensation in another. The second sensation arrives unasked. A sound might carry a color, or a taste a shape.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- what synesthesia is ::@:: The blending of two or more senses: one sense also produces a sensation in another.
- whether a synesthete chooses the second sensation ::@:: No, it arrives automatically.

## grapheme-color synesthesia

Grapheme-color synesthesia, the most common form, pairs a letter, digit, or word with a fixed color: 1 might be red and 2 orange. About 1 person in 200 has this form. Rarer forms join taste to color, or the sound of an instrument to a color.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- what grapheme-color synesthesia pairs ::@:: A letter, digit, or word with a color that stays the same each time.
- how common grapheme-color synesthesia is ::@:: About 1 person in 200.
- what the rarer forms of synesthesia join ::@:: Taste with color, or the sound of an instrument with a color.
