# nature versus nurture

The nature versus nurture question asks whether a person's development owes more to the genes they inherit or to the environment they grow up in. Nature is the biological endowment, and nurture is the social and physical environment beginning in the womb. Neither side works alone: genes and environment act on each other, so virtually every aspect of development comes from both.

- why the dichotomy of nature and nurture has limited relevance ::@:: Genes and environment act on each other in feedback loops, so neither side does a fixed share.
