# SOSC 1960 agent instructions

- The required readings are Noba Project modules from _Introduction to Psychology_, plus O'Toole and Ludvig (2026) for AI and psychology. The lecture slides are course material, so cite the readings in topic notes and never the slides.
- End every topic note with a `## references` section that states the attribution, then lists each external reading that contributed to the note: author, year, title, publication, link, and licence when the reading carries one, with a sub-bullet naming the sections that reading contributed to.
- Keep prose and cards source-neutral: attribution belongs in `## references`, so do not name a reading in the body and do not restate its licence wording there.
- A `lecture.md` holds the subject matter the session worked on. Everything about how the session ran belongs in the course `index.md` under `## logistics`, and none of it appears in the lecture note, in prose or in cards alike. Name the topics the session covered, since the note's sections follow them.
- A `lecture.md` is organised by problem set, not by question. A session's twenty questions arrive as four sets of five consecutive questions, so the note has one section per set: the set's questions quoted verbatim at the head, the answers written out together rather than one at a time, and a single card block at the end. A shorter session follows the same shape at its own size. Keep an example only when it is the clearest case or when it does something no other example does, and cut the rest. Where a topic note already defines a term, link it instead of restating it. Write the whole note short, and run the writing and humanizer passes over it: every sentence and every card earns its place or goes.
