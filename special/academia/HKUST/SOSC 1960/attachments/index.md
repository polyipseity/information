# index

The SOSC 1960 notes link to every figure here. Every figure is also embedded in the note that discusses it. Thirteen come from Wikimedia Commons. The other seven are SVGs from `generate_figures.py` beside this file, which records the searches behind them.

`Classical Conditioning.svg` shows all seven conditioning procedures in one chart. Two of them, extinction and blocking, appear there only as rows, so each also has a figure of its own.

## what each file is for

- [Blausen 0104 Brain x-secs SectionalPlanes.png](Blausen%200104%20Brain%20x-secs%20SectionalPlanes.png): three sectional planes drawn through a brain, each with the section it produces beside it, and the two names each of the planes carries.
- [Broca and Wernicke areas.svg](Broca%20and%20Wernicke%20areas.svg): the left side of a brain with Broca's and Wernicke's areas picked out, and the front and the back marked.
- [Classical Conditioning.svg](Classical%20Conditioning.svg): all seven procedures, each as a pair of rows: forward, simultaneous, second order, temporal, extinction, blocking, and inhibition.
- [ComponentsofERP.svg](ComponentsofERP.svg): labelled components of an event-related potential, with the polarity convention marked.
- [Gray 720-emphasizing-corpus-callosum.png](Gray%20720-emphasizing-corpus-callosum.png): a midsagittal section with the corpus callosum highlighted and arrowed, arching over the thalamus, with its genu, rostrum, and splenium labelled.
- [Human brain lateral view description.JPG](Human%20brain%20lateral%20view%20description.JPG): the left side of a brain, with the central and lateral sulci marked and the four lobes and the three poles numbered.
- [Operant conditioning diagram rev.svg](Operant%20conditioning%20diagram%20rev.svg): the consequences of a response, split by whether they change the response's frequency and whether a stimulus is added or removed.
- [PET Normal brain.jpg](PET%20Normal%20brain.jpg): one axial slice from a positron emission tomography scan, coloured region by region.
- [Phrenological chart Wellcome V0009523.jpg](Phrenological%20chart%20Wellcome%20V0009523.jpg): a Victorian broadside mapping faculties onto a symbolical head, with portraits of Gall and Spurzheim and heads typed moral and murderer's.
- [Skinner box scheme 01.svg](Skinner%20box%20scheme%2001.svg): the operant chamber and its mounted parts, with a subject inside.
- [SplitBrainExperiments.jpg](SplitBrainExperiments.jpg): a patient at a table facing a vertical screen on a stand, with objects beyond it and an examiner seated opposite.
- [Transcranial magnetic stimulation.jpg](Transcranial%20magnetic%20stimulation.jpg): a coil held over the scalp, with the pulsed field and the induced cortical current.
- [Visual Pathway to the Brain.jpg](Visual%20Pathway%20to%20the%20Brain.jpg): the pathway from both eyes to the occipital lobe, labelling the optic nerve, chiasm, tract, lateral geniculate nucleus, and optic radiations.

## generated figures

- [brain_division_naming.svg](brain_division_naming.svg): one brain under three naming schemes. Each structure box carries its embryological name, the forebrain, midbrain, and hindbrain brackets run down the right, and a dashed outline marks the brain stem cutting across both.
- [classical_conditioning_blocking.svg](classical_conditioning_blocking.svg): a blocking experiment's three phases, each showing which cue is present, and a test panel putting the trained cue beside the untrained one.
- [classical_conditioning_extinction.svg](classical_conditioning_extinction.svg): three rows showing a conditioned response stopping. Every row is a bell-alone trial with the meat withheld.
- [conditioning_relations.svg](conditioning_relations.svg): three relations around a behaviour: the occasion-setting arrow is dashed, the stimulus-control arrow solid.
- [correlation_causal_directions.svg](correlation_causal_directions.svg): three causal structures one correlation is compatible with, each row labelled.
- [observational_learning_stages.svg](observational_learning_stages.svg): the four stages of learning from a model. Their order is the point.
- [split_brain_test_setup.svg](split_brain_test_setup.svg): a screen with a fixation point and a stimulus flashed on one side, arrows from the stimulus to the right-hand half of each eye, two eyes split into nasal and temporal halves, and the hemispheres joined by a corpus callosum marked as cut.
