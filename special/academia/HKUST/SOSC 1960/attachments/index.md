## what each file is for

- [Blausen 0104 Brain x-secs SectionalPlanes.png](Blausen%200104%20Brain%20x-secs%20SectionalPlanes.png): three sectional planes through a brain, each with the section it produces beside it and the two names it carries.
- [Broca and Wernicke areas.svg](Broca%20and%20Wernicke%20areas.svg): the left side of a brain with Broca's and Wernicke's areas picked out, and the front and the back marked.
- [CAM and Grad-CAM example.png](CAM%20and%20Grad-CAM%20example.png): a photograph of a man, the convolutional layers that read it, the class score they return, and the same face under two heat maps, one from a class activation map and one from gradient-weighted class activation mapping.
- [Classical Conditioning.svg](Classical%20Conditioning.svg): all seven procedures, each as a pair of rows: forward, simultaneous, second order, temporal, extinction, blocking, and inhibition.
- [ComponentsofERP.svg](ComponentsofERP.svg): labelled components of an event-related potential, with the polarity convention marked.
- [Giuseppe Arcimboldo - Rudolf II of Habsburg as Vertumnus - Google Art Project.jpg](Giuseppe%20Arcimboldo%20-%20Rudolf%20II%20of%20Habsburg%20as%20Vertumnus%20-%20Google%20Art%20Project.jpg): a bust portrait built from fruit, vegetables, grain, and flowers, facing the viewer on a dark ground. Its nose is a pear, its lips cherries, its beard wheat, its hair grapes and a corn cob, its shoulders a gourd, a cauliflower, and an artichoke.
- [Gray 720-emphasizing-corpus-callosum.png](Gray%20720-emphasizing-corpus-callosum.png): a midsagittal section with the corpus callosum highlighted and arrowed, arching over the thalamus, and its genu, rostrum, and splenium labelled.
- [HermannGrid.svg](HermannGrid.svg): dark squares in a lattice, separated by white streets of even width, with the four sides of the grid cut off at the edge of the picture.
- [Human brain lateral view description.JPG](Human%20brain%20lateral%20view%20description.JPG): the left side of a brain, with the central and lateral sulci marked and the four lobes and the three poles numbered.
- [Operant conditioning diagram rev.svg](Operant%20conditioning%20diagram%20rev.svg): the consequences of a response, split by whether they change the response's frequency and whether a stimulus is added or removed.
- [PET Normal brain.jpg](PET%20Normal%20brain.jpg): one axial slice from a positron emission tomography scan, coloured region by region.
- [Phrenological chart Wellcome V0009523.jpg](Phrenological%20chart%20Wellcome%20V0009523.jpg): a Victorian broadside mapping faculties onto a symbolical head, with portraits of Gall and Spurzheim and heads typed moral and murderer's.
- [Skinner box scheme 01.svg](Skinner%20box%20scheme%2001.svg): the operant chamber and its mounted parts, with a subject inside.
- [SplitBrainExperiments.jpg](SplitBrainExperiments.jpg): a patient at a table facing a vertical screen on a stand, with objects beyond it and an examiner seated opposite.
- [Transcranial magnetic stimulation.jpg](Transcranial%20magnetic%20stimulation.jpg): a coil held over the scalp, with the pulsed field and the induced cortical current.
- [Visual Pathway to the Brain.jpg](Visual%20Pathway%20to%20the%20Brain.jpg): the pathway from both eyes to the occipital lobe, labelling the optic nerve, chiasm, tract, lateral geniculate nucleus, and optic radiations.

## figures searched on Commons

Each drawn figure below was checked against Wikimedia Commons first. The search ran on 2026-11-25, several query angles per figure, and every candidate named here was opened and looked at. The reason each was rejected is a fact the drawing contradicts, not a judgement about style.

- __[eye_section.svg](eye_section.svg)__ — `Schematic diagram of the human eye en.svg` (CC BY-SA 3.0) is cut off: its viewBox stops at 491 units, severing the cornea and pupil labels mid-word, and a black wedge covers the top of the section. `Blausen 0388 EyeAnatomy 01.png` (CC BY 3.0) is clean but views the eye from behind, so it carries neither the fovea nor the blind spot, leaves the aqueous and vitreous humours unlabelled, and draws no light path. The section is drawn because the note's material is the order light meets the parts and the inversion of the image.
- __[autonomic_nervous_system.svg](autonomic_nervous_system.svg)__ — `Sympathetic and Parasympathetic Nervous System.jpg` and `The Autonomic Nervous System.png` (both CC0) are the same spinal-wiring diagram: each lists constricted pupils, increased saliva, reduced heart rate, constricted bronchi, stimulated digestion, pancreas and gallbladder, constricted bladder and erection on one side against their opposites. Neither lists the blood vessels of the internal organs, which is one of the five organ pairs the note tabulates, and both encode the branching of the sympathetic chain, which the note does not cover.
- __[cognitive_science_hexagon.svg](cognitive_science_hexagon.svg)__ — `Cognitive Science Hexagon.svg` (CC BY-SA 3.0) draws a computer, a brain, a face, a mask and a colour chart inside a hexagon of triangles, and never names the hybrid fields. The hexagon is drawn to show which two disciplines produce each hybrid.
- __[stress_to_disease.svg](stress_to_disease.svg)__ — the searches returned only papers on stress, no diagram of this model.
