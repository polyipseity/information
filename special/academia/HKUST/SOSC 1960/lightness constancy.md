# lightness constancy

Lightness constancy is the tendency to see a surface as holding the same shade whatever light falls on it. A sheet of white paper looks white in sunlight and indoors, though the light reaching the eye differs by a large factor. The visual system needs the surface rather than the light falling on it. It estimates the illumination and discounts it.

## illusions

An illusion is the illumination estimate failing, which shows the system estimates at all. In the dress illusion, the pixels stayed the same, yet viewers split over whether the fabric was blue and black or white and gold. Each viewer's system had discounted a different light source. Perception is an inference, so an ambiguous image can resolve more than one way.
