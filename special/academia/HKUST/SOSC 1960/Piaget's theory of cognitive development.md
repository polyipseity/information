# Piaget's theory of cognitive development

Piaget's theory of cognitive development holds that children reason through four stages in a fixed order. A stage is a period in which a child reasons the same way about problems that look unrelated. A child who conserves quantity in one task conserves it in another. The stages differ in kind rather than in the amount a child knows, so the theory is a case of [discontinuous development](developmental%20stage%20theories.md).

- why Jean Piaget's theory is a discontinuous account of development ::@:: The stages differ in kind rather than in degree.

## stages and their reasoning

### four stages

The sensorimotor stage covers birth to about 2 years: the infant reasons through the senses and movement, and reaches [object permanence](object%20permanence.md) by the end. The preoperational stage covers about 2 to 6 or 7: the child uses symbols and words, but still reads appearance as reality. The concrete operational stage covers about 7 to 11: the child reasons logically about what is in front of them, and conserves quantity, mass, and volume. The formal operational stage covers about 12 onward, and adds reasoning about the possible: deductive inference, hypothetical cases, and systematic tests of an idea.

### conservation

Conservation is the understanding that a quantity stays the same when its container, shape, or apparent size changes. The usual test pours a fixed amount of liquid from a short wide glass into a tall thin one and asks whether there is now more, less, or the same. A child who answers that the taller glass holds more has read the change in appearance as a change in amount. The preoperational stage fails this test, and the concrete operational stage passes it.

### reversibility

Reversibility is the understanding that an operation can be undone: the poured liquid can go back into the short glass, and the amount returns to what it was. The child can run the transformation backwards, so the change in appearance no longer fools them. Conservation and reversibility arrive together in the concrete operational stage.
