# visual neurotechnology

Visual neurotechnology runs in two directions. A retinal prosthesis writes a visual signal into the brain of a blind person. Mind reading recovers an image from the brain instead. No artificial system can yet see and understand light the way a person does.

---

- the two directions of visual neurotechnology ::@:: Writing a visual signal into the brain, and recovering an image from it.
- what no artificial system can yet do with light ::@:: See and understand it the way a person does.

## retinal prosthesis

A retinal prosthesis is a set of photosensitive circuits implanted on the back of a blind person's eyeball. The circuits connect to the visual areas of the brain, so the signal arrives there without passing through a working retina. The patient gets a partial visual experience, not a restored eye.

- where a retinal prosthesis sends its signal ::@:: To the visual areas of the brain, without passing through a working retina.
- what a retinal prosthesis gives a blind person ::@:: A partial visual experience, not a restored eye.

## mind reading

Mind reading recovers an image from the brain instead of delivering one to it. [Functional magnetic resonance imaging](functional%20magnetic%20resonance%20imaging.md) has decoded a sleeping person's dream images from brain activity.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- what mind reading recovers ::@:: An image from the brain, rather than delivering one to it.
- how the dream images were decoded ::@:: From brain activity, using functional magnetic resonance imaging.
