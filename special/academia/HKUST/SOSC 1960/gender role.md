# gender role

Your work, how you speak, how you react, the habits you are supposed to have. A gender role is what a society expects of you because of your sex.

A role most people share is a stereotype. It says what people expect of a sex, not what its members are like. Believing one is a claim about a group, not evidence about the people in it.

## stereotyped behaviour

She comes home to cooking, washing, vacuuming, and cleaning. He rests, watches television, and sleeps.

She talks longer and wanders more. His talk turns into a contest, hers overlaps. She cries at a romantic film that sends him to sleep. She spends hours on an appearance he settles in seconds.

He travels the world with ease, while she stays tied to the house. He packs one item, she fills the car with luggage. He walks straight to what he came for, she works through every aisle.

Both sexes want the same partner. That partner stands on a pedestal among cooking, cleaning, laundry, and childcare.

Everyday stereotypes divide life along sex.
