# vestibulo-ocular reflex

The vestibulo-ocular reflex turns the eyes against the head's motion, by the same amount, so gaze stays on its target. It pairs vision with what the vestibular system reports about the head's position and motion.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- what the vestibulo-ocular reflex does ::@:: It moves the eyes opposite to the head, by the same amount, so gaze stays on its target.
- which two senses the vestibulo-ocular reflex pairs ::@:: Vision and the vestibular sense of head motion.
- what the vestibular system reports ::@:: The motion and position of the body and head.

## keeping an image sharp

A moving eye smears a still object across the retina, so a steady image takes compensation. While you hold a finger and turn your head, the reflex keeps the finger's image on the same retinal spot. Wave the finger with the head still, and nothing compensates, so the finger blurs.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- why a still object blurs when the eye moves ::@:: The object's image smears across the retina, and nothing compensates for the eye's movement.
- why a finger stays sharp while you turn your head ::@:: The vestibulo-ocular reflex keeps the finger's image on the same retinal spot.
