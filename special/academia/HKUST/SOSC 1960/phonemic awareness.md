# phonemic awareness

Phonemic awareness is the awareness of phonemes, the individual sounds that make up a spoken word. A child with it knows that "rain" is built from the r, ay, and n sounds. It is part of phonological awareness, which also covers larger units such as syllables and the onset and rime of a syllable. Phonics teaching builds on it, and children need it to learn to read. Assessment asks a child whether two words rhyme, whether they begin with the same sound, or which sounds a word contains.

- how phonemic awareness differs from phonological awareness ::@:: Phonemic awareness covers phonemes alone; phonological awareness also covers syllables and the onset and rime.
- how phonemic awareness is measured ::@:: By asking whether two words rhyme, whether they begin with the same sound, or which sounds a word contains.
