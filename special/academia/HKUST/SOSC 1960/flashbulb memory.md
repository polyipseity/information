# flashbulb memory

Flashbulb memory describes the moment a person receives significant news that seems to etch their memory with great clarity. The events that produce one are emotional and distinctive: rare or distinctive events catch attention and are remembered better, as when someone sees a giraffe on campus. A collective flashbulb moment is news a group receives at once: what were you doing when you first learned of the Tai Po fire tragedy? People often have great confidence in their memories of such moments, although those memories are not as accurate.
