# two-streams hypothesis

Visual information leaving the [primary visual cortex](visual%20cortex.md) splits into two streams. The ventral stream works out what an object is, and the dorsal stream works out where it is and how to act on it.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- what the two visual streams divide between them ::@:: Recognizing what an object is, and locating it so you can act on it.
- which stream recognizes objects ::@:: The ventral stream, also called the what pathway.

## ventral stream

The ventral stream, also called the what pathway, runs along the underside of the brain and carries out recognition. Its areas specialize: one for faces, which take fine discrimination to tell apart, another for places and scenes, a third for letters and words. It assigns an edge to the object it belongs to, separates a figure from its background, and gives a surface its color. [Specialised areas](visual%20cortex.md#specialised%20areas) names each one.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- which pathway recognizes faces ::@:: The ventral stream, whose face area handles fine discrimination.
- which ventral job besides faces and places has an area of its own ::@:: Telling letters and words apart.
- how the ventral stream handles edges and surfaces ::@:: It assigns edges to the object they belong to and gives surfaces their color.

## dorsal stream

The dorsal stream, also called the where-and-how pathway, carries the location and motion of things, including your own body's motion through a scene. It lets you reach for an object, navigate an environment, or step around an obstacle without deciding what it is.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- what the where-and-how pathway computes ::@:: The location and motion of things, and how to act on them.
- which pathway lets you avoid an obstacle ::@:: The where-and-how pathway, the dorsal stream.
- what the dorsal stream's motion information is used for ::@:: Reaching for an object, navigating an environment, and avoiding obstacles.
- what the dorsal stream computes about your own body ::@:: Its motion through a scene.
