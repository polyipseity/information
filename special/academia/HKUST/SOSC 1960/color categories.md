# color categories

A color category groups the shades that a language treats as one color. Where those boundaries fall varies by culture, and the variation shows in color sensitivity: one culture separates shades that another sees as one color.

How the brain sorts colors depends on language and cultural learning. Brain imaging shows that learning a new color category increases white-matter volume. The effect on how people use and experience color is small but definite.

## category boundaries

The Berinmo of New Guinea put the greens of living leaves and the greens of dying leaves in two color categories, though a Western speaker sees one green. Russian speakers put light blue and dark blue in two categories, though most Western speakers see one blue.
