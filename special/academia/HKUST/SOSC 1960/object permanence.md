# object permanence

Object permanence is the understanding that an object continues to exist when it is out of sight. Jean Piaget placed its acquisition at the end of the [sensorimotor stage](Piaget's%20theory%20of%20cognitive%20development.md), by about two years of age. An experimenter tests it by hiding a toy and watching whether the infant searches for it. An infant who does not search gives no sign of knowing the toy is still there.
