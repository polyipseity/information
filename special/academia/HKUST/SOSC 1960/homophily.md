# homophily

Homophily is the tendency for people to form communities with similar others.

[Artificial intelligence](artificial%20intelligence.md) (AI) chatbots mimic individual human behaviour in psychological and economic tasks. A large-scale simulation found that groups of them mimic collective behaviour too. Communities form over time around bots using a common language and, among bots that predominantly use English, around bots posting similar content (He et al., 2024). Homophily among chatbots may let social science simulate nuanced aspects of collective behaviour.
