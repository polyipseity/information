# need for closure

The need for closure is the wish to end ambiguity and reach a definite decision. Time pressure raises it. So does discomfort with ambiguity, which varies from one person to the next (Webster & Kruglanski, 1997). A hungry group takes the nearest restaurant without weighing which is best.
