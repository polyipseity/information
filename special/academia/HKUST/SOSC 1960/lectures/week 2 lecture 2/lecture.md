## reading a study for a question

> __Prompt.__ How would keeping a pet help elderly feel less lonely? Spot the keywords.

The common answer was companionship, with a sense of purpose alongside it. That is the mechanism, while the animatronic-pet program measured the outcome: less loneliness and better well-being. Its design turned on a feasibility test, with surveys before and after the intervention. Randomized trials are still owed before the evidence is definitive.

---

## paraphrasing a finding

> __Prompt.__ Rewrite the finding in your own words: "Momentary distractions by one's cellphone during time spent with a significant other likely lowers the significant other's satisfaction with their relationship, and could lead to enhanced feelings of depression and lower well-being of that individual." (Baylor University, 2015)

Most rewrites used the writer's own words and named the authors. The failures were the source's wording kept nearly whole with the citation attached, or a changed claim.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- how a rewrite changed the claim ::@:: A wrong year, or a partner finding widened to one about everyone.
