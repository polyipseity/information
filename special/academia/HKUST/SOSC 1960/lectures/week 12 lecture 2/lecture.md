# lecture

- HKUST SOSC 1960 week 12 lecture 2
- parent: [week 12 lecture 2](index.md)

Health psychology covers the physiology of stress in the general adaptation syndrome, stress and memory, coping and resilience, adherence to medical advice, control in later life, biofeedback, and self-efficacy. Health psychologists define health through the biopsychosocial model, which views a person's overall health as a very complex recipe with many different ingredients. Psychoneuroimmunology is the research field that studies how academic stress compromises the body's ability to fight viruses.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- the ten topics health psychology covers ::@:: Resilience, biofeedback, adherence, coping, stress and memory, the biopsychosocial model, psychoneuroimmunology, the general adaptation syndrome, control in later life, and self-efficacy.
- what the ability to bounce back from a challenge is called ::@:: Resilience.
- what learning to control ordinarily involuntary functions such as heart rate and blood pressure by watching machines that show them is called ::@:: Biofeedback.
- the current term for the tendency to do as a physician suggests about health-related behaviour, such as taking medications or stopping alcohol or tobacco, and the term it replaced ::@:: Adherence, which replaced compliance.
- which kind of coping is shown by asking a team to come to work on Saturday so that a deadline is met ::@:: Problem-focused coping.
- the relation between stress and memory performance, and the level of stress under which performance is best ::@:: An inverted U: a little stress aids recall and a lot impairs it, so a moderate level is best.
- which model health psychologists generally define health by, and what it views a person's overall health as ::@:: The biopsychosocial model, which views a person's overall health as a very complex recipe with many different ingredients.
- which field research contributes to when it studies how academic stress compromises the body's ability to fight viruses ::@:: Psychoneuroimmunology.
- the three stages of the general adaptation syndrome in order, and what each does ::@:: Alarm, which mobilizes the body's physiological stress-response mechanisms; resistance, which copes with the stress that is being experienced; exhaustion, in which the body exhausts its ability to fight the stress and develops an illness.
- how to enhance the quality of life of older adults who reside in an assisted living facility ::@:: Give them control over as many decisions that affect them as possible.
- what the belief that one can perform adequately in a given situation or circumstance is called ::@:: Self-efficacy.
