# lecture

- HKUST SOSC 1960 week 11 lecture 2
- parent: [week 11 lecture 2](index.md)

Social cognition covers schemas, heuristics, mood-congruent memory, affective forecasting, motivated skepticism, need for closure, and explicit and implicit attitudes.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- what social cognition covers ::@:: Schemas, heuristics, mood-congruent memory, affective forecasting, motivated skepticism, need for closure, and explicit and implicit attitudes.
- which concept explains why a person who is currently sad can more easily recall a time when they got in trouble for a late assignment ::@:: Mood-congruent memory.
- which heuristic a person likely uses when asked whether the English language has more words that start with the letter "k" or more words with "k" as the third letter ::@:: The availability heuristic.
- how schemas save a person time when meeting a new experience, person, or object ::@:: The new encounter is compared to a previously stored schema, so assessing it takes less effort.
- which concept a patient's doubt about a doctor's test result illustrates when the patient is motivated to view herself in a positive light ::@:: Motivated skepticism.
- what the desire to come to a decision that resolves and concludes an issue is called ::@:: The need for closure.
- which bias makes a person overestimate how long a feeling might last, and which kind of forecasting it belongs to ::@:: The durability bias, a type of affective forecasting.
- the bias shown by a person who predicts immense happiness at an event but feels less happiness than predicted ::@:: The impact bias.
- what the mental shortcuts people use to simplify their social world are called ::@:: Heuristics.
- the kind of attitudes a self-report survey measures when it asks people to fill out their attitudes directly ::@:: Explicit attitudes.
- what a psychological tendency expressed by evaluating a target with a degree of favor or disfavor is ::@:: An attitude.

> __Question 1.__ Mood-congruent memory is occurring in which of the following scenarios?
>
> 1. Jasmine is feeling anxious but is having a difficult time recalling how nervous she was to give a public speech.
> 2. John is currently sad and can more easily recall a time when he got in trouble for a late assignment.
> 3. The sunny day is making Jaden happy, helping him to recall what he ate for breakfast this morning when he was in a hurry.

> - solution: {@{2. John is currently sad and can more easily recall a time when he got in trouble for a late assignment.}@}
