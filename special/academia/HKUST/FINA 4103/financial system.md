# financial system

The financial system governs how financial contracts are issued and exchanged. It spans banks and large financial institutions, financial markets, the participants who trade in them, and the regulation that makes those contracts enforceable, and it is highly heterogeneous.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- overview ::@:: The set of institutions, markets, and rules that governs how financial contracts are issued and exchanged.
- what the financial system governs ::@:: How financial contracts are issued and exchanged.
- a defining property of the financial system ::@:: It is highly heterogeneous.

## financial markets and institutions

The system is built from banks and large financial institutions, and from the financial markets where contracts change hands.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- what the financial system is built from ::@:: Institutions, chiefly banks and large financial institutions, and the financial markets where contracts change hands.

## market participants

Market participants are those who get involved in financial transactions. Banks and large financial institutions take part as brokers and dealers, retail investors and households trade on their own account, and governments issue and trade. The population is highly heterogeneous, and many constraints limit what each participant may do.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- who counts as a market participant ::@:: Anyone who gets involved in financial transactions: banks and large financial institutions, retail investors and households, and governments.
- role of banks and large financial institutions as participants ::@:: Brokers and dealers.
- a defining property of the set of market participants ::@:: It is highly heterogeneous, with many constraints on what each participant can do.

## regulation

Regulation exists to make sure financial contracts are enforced, so the legal apparatus that backs a contract is part of the financial system.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- why the financial system needs regulation ::@:: To make sure financial contracts are enforced.
