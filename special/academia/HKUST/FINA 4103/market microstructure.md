# market microstructure

Market microstructure is the branch of finance that analyses how different market structures affect market quality.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- overview ::@:: The branch of finance that analyses how different market structures affect market quality.

## market quality

Market quality is what a structure is judged by, and it rests on two pillars. The first is _liquidity_, the ability to trade without paying much or moving the price. The second is _price discovery_, also called price efficiency, the speed with which the price incorporates information.

---

Flashcards for this section are as follows:

- the two pillars of market quality ::@:: Liquidity and price discovery.
- first pillar of market quality ::@:: Liquidity: the ability to trade without paying much or moving the price.
- second pillar of market quality ::@:: Price discovery, also called price efficiency: how quickly the price incorporates new information.

## market-based evaluation

Comparing market structures needs a metric for their impact. The natural candidate is welfare, taken over traders, dealers, exchanges, and firms, but it is typically not observable. Microstructure therefore takes a market-based approach and reads the impact off asset prices and other market metrics, interpreted through the efficient-market hypothesis.
