# index

- HKUST FINA 4103
- name: Financial Markets Trading and Structure
- credits: 3

---

FINA 4103 is an undergraduate course in financial markets trading and structure at HKUST covering liquidity, information, market structure, and trading. It asks how trading systems and markets perform, how algorithmic trading and intermediaries change them, and how regulation and emerging technologies such as blockchain and decentralization reshape market structure. Alternative market structures are compared by their economic and operational effectiveness through canonical market microstructure theory.

## overview

- official course outline
    - introduction
    - overview of general market structures
    - orders and quotes
    - players in financial markets
    - US market structure and trading rules
    - platform architectures
    - microstructure data issues
    - information in finance
    - models of price dynamics
    - measuring liquidity
    - order size and strategic trading
    - high-frequency trading
    - decentralized market
- intended learning outcomes
    - incorporate the notion of information in analyses of financial markets
    - measure market liquidity theoretically and empirically
    - understand financial market details and trading structures
    - understand the impact of recent developments in financial technologies
    - analyse market quality under different market structures, trading rules, and market policies
- notes
    - the first half, before the midterm, covers institutional details of various markets, and the second half covers theoretical frameworks that describe and measure key market features and evaluate market regulations and policies
    - a background in statistics (random variables, expectation, variance, covariance, sample versus population data, linear regression) and in algebra, calculus, and optimization (economic models of financial markets, first-order derivatives) is expected
    - the published class schedule is tentative and subject to change
