# index

- HKUST COMP 4900
- name: Academic and Professional Development
- credits: 0
- status: recurrent

---

COMP 4900 is a zero-credit academic and professional development course required for the COMP and COSC curricula at HKUST. It is graded P, PP, or F and runs alongside every term a student is registered in CSE. Requirements differ between the first term, the in-between terms, and the final term. Activities include seminars, workshops, advising and sharing sessions, and discussion with faculty, teaching staff, and fellow students or alumni.

## logistics

- requirements: <https://cse.hkust.edu.hk/ug/comp4900/>
- grading: PP (permitted to proceed) each term; P (pass) in the final term before graduation; F for failure
- sections:
    - tutorials: \[missing\]
        - T1: LTA; WednesdayT18:00:00+08:00/WednesdayT19:00:00+08:00
        - T2: LTA; WednesdayT18:00:00+08:00/WednesdayT19:00:00+08:00
        - T3: LTA; WednesdayT18:00:00+08:00/WednesdayT19:00:00+08:00
- note: the T1–T3 tutorials meet together for the Wednesday 18:00–19:00 seminars
- note: attendance is valid only after arriving before 18:10 and taking attendance with the TA
- note: a seminar that requires a reflection report counts only when the report is submitted within one week of the seminar, is at least 200 words, and earns a Pass
- note: one ENGG 2010 seminar per term can be double-counted, but only for a student officially registered to ENGG 2010
- note: COMP students attend two seminars and an advising meeting each in-between term; COSC students attend one seminar
- note: the first term in CSE adds the advisor welcome meeting, the department UG orientation, and the training modules; the last term adds the compulsory graduation survey
- note: a term with leave status has no registration and no COMP 4900 requirement
- note: COMP 4900 is not offered in the summer term, so a Summer graduation needs the P grade in the preceding Spring
- note: double failure of COMP 4900 can make a student subject to dismissal

## overview

- intended learning outcomes
    - develop relationships with faculty members and staff
    - examine the value of and assume ownership of one's education
    - become self-directed in planning and accomplishing short- and long-term academic goals
    - identify short- and long-term career and personal plans and aspirations, and work towards them by establishing realistic development plans
- notes
    - COMP and COSC differ in the number of required seminars and training modules
