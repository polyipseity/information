# ELEC 2400 agent instructions

- Every figure lives on a flashcard, not only in the prose. A card that describes a circuit in words will not bring the circuit back.
- A recognition card puts the drawing in the prompt and asks what it shows. A recall card asks the reader to draw it and puts the drawing in the answer. Every drawing needs at least one recall card.
- A note with several figures gets a card for each one. One card cannot stand for two different pictures. Alt text describes the drawing in plain words, with no LaTeX.
- A figure and its card go in together.
