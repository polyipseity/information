# MGMT 2010 agent instructions

## course character

This is an experiential, discussion-based course. Classes are face-to-face with a mandatory "no screens" policy for the first 30 minutes and during quizzes/team activities. There are no recordings available.

## the course's core insight

The central thesis of MGMT 2010 is that people are not inherently "good" or "bad" — the environment and incentives they face affect how ethically they act. Every lecture, poll, and in-class activity demonstrates this through the gap between stated values and actual behaviour. When ingesting material, extract this insight. Do not catalogue poll percentages or activity descriptions as standalone content; instead, fold the evidence into the concept it illustrates.

## what counts as durable content

- __Lecture slides__: the ethical frameworks (duty, utilitarian, virtue, caring, egoism, justice), Carroll's CSR model, the ethics/morals distinction, the moral compass spectrum — these are conceptual foundations.
- __Polls__: the data is transient, but the patterns it reveals (self-serving bias, secular ethics assumptions, the gap between self-rated and perceived honesty) are durable course concepts. Fold them into the relevant topic note.
- __In-class activities__: the questionnaire, the AI trolley problem, and the values game are teaching instruments. Their purpose is to surface the course's core insight. Record what they teach, not what they contain.

## reconciliation rule

Every ingestion must be compared against existing topic notes. A session file is never the final home of a concept. Extract the insight, fold it into the owning note, and leave the session entry lean.
